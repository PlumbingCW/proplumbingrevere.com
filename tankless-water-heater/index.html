Tankless Water Heater in Revere, MA

A tankless water heater in Revere, MA heats water on demand instead of storing it, so it needs the right gas or electric supply, venting and regular maintenance. We install new units, repair error codes and flush heat exchangers.

Basics

How a tankless unit works and where it fits

A tankless heater fires a burner or energizes elements only when you open a hot tap. That removes standby loss and gives continuous hot water, but the unit must deliver a big temperature rise in seconds. A gas unit therefore needs a bigger gas pipe than a tank does.

In Revere, where many buildings have small gas lines and old chimneys, the first step is a supply and venting check. A heater that cannot get enough gas will trip off at high demand and leave you with lukewarm water.

Problems

Common tankless faults we repair

Error codes

Codes point to ignition, flame, flow or venting faults. The plumber reads the code and tests the part behind it.

Lukewarm water at high flow

Usually a gas supply or scale problem, or a mixing valve that needs adjusting.

Cold sandwich effect

A burst of cold water between uses. Recirculation or a buffer tank can help.

Clogged inlet screen

Debris after water main work can block the screen and cut flow.

Before you call

What to do when a tankless heater stops

Read the display

Write down any error code. It tells the plumber which part to test first.

Check power and gas

Confirm the unit has power and the gas valve is open. Do not relight or reset it more than once.

Look for water

A drip at the unit or its pipes means shut the cold supply valve and call.

Smell gas? Leave

Leave the building and call 911 or National Grid at 1-800-233-5325. Do not use switches or phones inside.

Maintenance

How a tankless flush is done

Manufacturers recommend periodic descaling. According to the MWRA, its water is soft, at about 16 mg/L of hardness, so scale usually builds more slowly than in hard-water towns.

1

Isolate the unit

Service valves are closed and the heater is powered down.

2

Circulate a descaling solution

A pump runs a mild acid through the heat exchanger for the time the maker specifies.

3

Rinse and clean the screen

The unit is flushed with clean water and the inlet filter is cleaned.

4

Test

The plumber checks flow, temperature and error history.

Permit

Gas piping, venting and permits

A tankless gas unit often needs a larger gas pipe, a new vent and a permit. In Massachusetts, master and journeyman plumbers may also do gas fitting, and the Revere Building Division issues plumbing and gas permits only to licensed plumbers and gas fitters.

Never let an unlicensed installer connect gas. A poor vent can let carbon monoxide into the house. Install a working carbon monoxide detector near sleeping areas whatever the heater type.

FAQ

Common questions

How often should a tankless water heater be flushed?

Many makers say once a year. Revere's soft MWRA water usually means slower scale buildup, but the flush still checks the screen and the heat exchanger.

Can my gas line support a tankless heater?

Maybe. Tankless units draw more gas at once than tanks. The plumber measures the line and meter before recommending a model.

Do you repair tankless heaters made by any brand?

We repair common brands. Parts for older or rare models can take longer to find.

Is tankless better than a heat pump water heater?

They serve different goals. Tankless gives endless hot water on demand. A heat pump water heater cuts electricity use and may earn a Mass Save rebate.

Do you do tankless emergency repair at night?

Yes, the line is answered 24/7 and a plumber can be sent when available. See water heater repair for the full process.

How long does a tankless install take?

A swap on existing connections can take a day. A new gas pipe or vent can take longer.
