Repair a Porcelain Toilet Bowl in Revere, MA: What Works

A small chip on the outside of a toilet bowl is cosmetic and can be filled with a porcelain repair kit. A crack that runs below the water line, or that leaks, cannot be repaired safely and the toilet should be replaced.

Do not rely on glue or epoxy to hold a cracked bowl. A failed repair floods a floor, and in an apartment it can reach the unit below.

    Cosmetic repair

    How to fix a small chip

    1

    Clean and dry

    Wipe the chip with alcohol and let it dry fully.

    2

    Fill

    Apply porcelain filler in thin layers with a small tool.

    3

    Cure

    Let each layer cure for the time on the package.

    4

    Sand and polish

    Smooth the area gently so it feels flush.

    Leaks

    How to tell a crack from condensation

    A cold tank in a warm bathroom sweats, and the water can look like a leak. Dry the outside with a towel, then place dry paper along the base and under the tank. If it is wet again in an hour with no use, you have a leak.

    Add a few drops of food dye to the tank and watch for color on the paper. Colored water at the base points to a crack or a bad seal.

    Local note

    Local note: rental housing and health rules in Revere

    According to the City of Revere housing plan, about 51 percent of households rent. In a rental, a leaking or cracked toilet is usually the landlord's responsibility, and tenants should report it in writing.

    The Massachusetts housing code at 105 CMR 410.000 sets minimum standards for rental housing, including working plumbing and fixtures. A tenant who cannot get a repair can ask the Revere health or inspectional services office what steps are available.

    Do not use a toilet with a leaking bowl. Water on the floor can rot the subfloor and ceiling below.

    Sources: Massachusetts housing code 105 CMR 410.000 (state regulation) and the City of Revere housing plan (municipal source).

    Replacing

    What replacement involves

    A plumber shuts off the supply, removes the toilet, inspects the flange and sets a new toilet on a new wax ring. Most visits are short, and the old unit is hauled away if you ask.

    If the floor is stained or soft, the plumber may need to repair it first. For a leak that reaches walls, see leak detection, and our Chelsea page covers the nearby city.

    Safety

    Why a cracked bowl is a safety issue

    A cracked bowl can leak slowly and soak the floor for weeks before anyone notices. In a multi-unit building the water can reach the ceiling below. A chip near the rim can also leave a sharp edge.

    Stop using a toilet with a leaking bowl. Close the shutoff valve behind it and call a plumber. Tell your landlord in writing if you rent.

    Cleaning and care

    How to keep porcelain from chipping or cracking

    Avoid dropping heavy items on the bowl or tank lid. Do not tighten bolts hard with a long wrench, because porcelain breaks under uneven force. Skip harsh abrasives that dull the glaze, because a rough surface stains and traps grime.

    Check the base for movement every few months. A toilet that rocks puts stress on the bowl and can lead to cracks.

    Choosing a new toilet

    What to buy if you replace it

    Measure the rough-in from the wall to the center of the drain. Common sizes are 10, 12 and 14 inches, and the new toilet must match. Check whether you want a round or elongated bowl and measure the space in front of it.

    Choose a model with a good flush rating and a trap that is easy to clear. A comfort height seat is taller and may suit older residents. Ask the plumber to bring the toilet or tell you the model so the visit is not delayed.

    Temporary steps

    What to do until the repair visit

    Close the shutoff valve behind the toilet and flush once to empty the tank. Place a towel and a bucket nearby, and tape a note to the lid so no one uses it. Use another bathroom if you have one.

    If you must use it, close the valve after each use and open it only to refill. This is a short stopgap, not a plan. Mop up any water right away so the floor does not stain or warp.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Can I use epoxy on a cracked toilet bowl?

    Epoxy can hide a crack but will not hold under flushing pressure. Replace a bowl with a crack below the water line.

    Is a hairline crack dangerous?

    It can be harmless for a while, but it can grow. Watch it and plan to replace.

    Who pays for a cracked toilet in a rental?

    Often the landlord. Report it in writing and keep a copy.

    Does the Massachusetts housing code mention plumbing?

    The state sanitary code at 105 CMR 410.000 sets minimum standards for housing, including fixtures. Ask the city if you need help.

    Can a plumber repair a cracked tank?

    Usually not. A cracked tank is replaced, sometimes with the bowl if parts are not made.
