Garbage Disposal Installation in Revere, MA: A Homeowner Guide

Installing a garbage disposal means mounting a flange under the sink, hanging the unit, connecting it to the drain trap and wiring or plugging it in. If you are swapping a disposal that is already there, most of the hard part is done.

A first-time install needs a power source under the sink and a drain that is in good condition. If either is missing, a plumber and an electrician may need to work together.

    Overview

    The main steps

    1

    Power off

    Switch off the circuit and test it. Remove the old unit if there is one.

    2

    Mount the flange

    Seat the sink flange with plumber's putty and tighten the mounting ring.

    3

    Hang the disposal

    Lift the unit onto the mount and lock it in place.

    4

    Connect the drain

    Attach the discharge tube to the trap, connect the dishwasher hose if there is one and restore power.

    Before you buy

    What to check first

    Power under the sink

    You need an outlet or a switched circuit. Do not run a cord from elsewhere.

    Dishwasher knockout

    If you have a dishwasher, the plug in the disposal inlet must be removed so water can drain.

    Trap and drain

    Old or corroded pipe may need replacing.

    Room under the sink

    Measure depth. Larger motors need more space.

    Local note

    Local note: your sewer lateral and what goes down it

    The Revere Water and Sewer Department states that the owner is responsible for the sewer service pipe from the house to the city main. A grease clog or blockage on your lateral is yours to clear.

    The department also prohibits connecting downspouts, sump pumps and other surface or groundwater sources to the sewer. The city recorded more than 722 blockage-related basement backups since 2000 under the EPA consent decree, which is one reason grease control matters.

    Call the department at 781-286-8145 if you are not sure where the city main ends and your pipe begins.

    Sources: City of Revere Water and Sewer Department pages and EPA consent decree summary (municipal and federal sources).

    Jams

    What to do when it jams

    Turn off the power first. Use the hex slot on the bottom of the unit to turn the blades by hand, then press the reset button. Never put your hand in the opening.

    If a slow drain remains after the unit runs, the trap or the line beyond may be clogged. See drain cleaning, and our Chelsea page if you are next door.

    Choosing a unit

    How to choose a disposal for your kitchen

    Disposals come in different motor sizes. A smaller motor suits a single person or a couple who cook little. A larger motor suits a family that cooks daily. Continuous feed units run while the switch is on, and batch feed units run only when the stopper is in place.

    Check the sink opening and the space under it before buying. Some units are quiet because of added insulation, and some have a stainless grinding chamber that resists rust.

    Standards

    What the plumbing code expects

    According to the Massachusetts plumbing code, a disposal needs a proper trap and a drain that vents correctly. A plumber checks that the trap, the tailpiece and the connection to the dishwasher meet code.

    If the sink drain is old or slow, fix it first. A new disposal on a bad drain only moves the clog to a different spot.

    Care

    Daily habits that protect the unit

    Run cold water before, during and after use. Grind small amounts at a time. Clean the unit now and then with ice and a little citrus peel or a cleaner made for disposals. Never pour grease into it, and never use chemical drain openers, which can damage the seals.

    Rental kitchens

    If you rent

    Ask the landlord before you install or remove a disposal. Many leases require the landlord to approve changes to plumbing and wiring. If the disposal breaks, report it in writing with a photo and the date.

    Do not run a replacement disposal on a shared circuit if the wiring looks old. A licensed electrician should check it.

    Odors

    How to stop a disposal from smelling

    Food film builds up under the splash guard and on the chamber walls. Scrub the guard with a brush and dish soap. Grind ice cubes and a few citrus peels to clean the walls, then flush with cold water.

    If the smell stays, the drain trap may be dirty or the vent may be blocked. A plumber can check both and clear them.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Can a homeowner install a garbage disposal?

    Often yes for a replacement. A first-time install needs power and drain work, so many homeowners call a plumber.

    Is a garbage disposal allowed in Revere?

    Revere does not ban them in the sources we reviewed, but the owner is responsible for the lateral. Ask the Water and Sewer Department if you have concerns.

    Why does my disposal hum and not spin?

    The blades are jammed. Turn off power, use the hex slot to free them and press the reset button.

    Do I need to remove the dishwasher knockout?

    Yes, if a dishwasher drains into the disposal. Skipping it causes dishwasher backups.

    Can I put grease down a disposal?

    No. Grease hardens in the line and is a common cause of sewer backups.
