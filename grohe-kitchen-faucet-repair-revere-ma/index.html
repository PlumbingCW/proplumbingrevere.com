Grohe Kitchen Faucet Repair in Revere, MA Homes

Most Grohe kitchen faucet problems come from three parts. A worn cartridge causes drips at the spout or leaks around the handle. A clogged aerator causes weak or sideways flow. A frayed or stuck hose causes a pull-down spray that will not retract.

Find the model number first, because cartridges and hoses differ by model. Shut off the supply under the sink before you open anything.

    Diagnosis

    Match the symptom to the part

    Handle leaks and spout drips point to the cartridge. A cartridge is the cylinder inside the faucet body that controls flow and temperature. When its seals wear, water slips past them.

    Weak flow, a spray that splits or water that runs sideways points to the aerator, the small screen at the spout tip. Unscrew it and look for debris. A pull-down hose that jams or hangs low points to the hose, the counterweight or the docking magnet.

    Cartridge swap

    How a cartridge replacement works

    The exact steps depend on the model. Check the instruction sheet that came with the faucet.

    1

    Shut off water

    Close both supply valves under the sink and open the faucet to release pressure.

    2

    Remove the handle

    Pop the cap, remove the screw and lift the handle off.

    3

    Pull the cartridge

    Loosen the retaining nut or clip and pull the old cartridge straight out.

    4

    Fit the new one

    Match the part number, seat it with new O-rings and reassemble.

    Local note

    Local note: old brass faucets, pH and what the MWRA says

    According to the MWRA, its reservoir water is lead-free, and lead can enter tap water from lead service lines, lead solder or older brass faucets. The MWRA adjusts the pH of treated water to about 9.0 to 9.5 to reduce the tendency of copper and lead to leach from household plumbing.

    If your faucet is old, repairing a drip is a good moment to ask whether replacement makes more sense than another cartridge. Ask the Revere Water Department at 781-286-8145 or the MWRA at 617-242-5323 about testing your tap.

    Revere water is also soft, at about 16 mg/L of hardness, so mineral scale is a less common reason for a stuck aerator here than in hard-water towns. Look for debris instead.

    Sources: MWRA 2025 drinking water results and the MWRA hardness page (utility source).

    Repair or replace

    When to stop repairing and replace

    Replace a faucet when the body is corroded, the finish is flaking, parts are no longer made or the repair cost is close to a new faucet. Replace the shutoff valves and supply lines at the same time if they are old.

    If water is spraying and you cannot close the shutoff, close the main and call. Leak detection helps if the cabinet is wet and you cannot find the source, and our Chelsea service covers the next city over.

    Parts

    Getting the right replacement part

    Grohe lists parts by model and part number, and many faucets look alike but use different cartridges. Photograph the faucet, the base and any markings under the sink before you shop. Take the old cartridge with you if you buy in person.

    Buy O-rings and a small tube of silicone grease at the same time. Fresh seals and a thin coat of grease make a new cartridge last longer and turn more smoothly. Do not use petroleum grease on rubber parts unless the maker approves it.

    Prevention

    Habits that keep a faucet working longer

    Clean the aerator every few months. Wipe the finish with a soft cloth and mild soap rather than harsh cleaners. Close the handle gently instead of forcing it. These small habits slow cartridge wear and protect the finish.

    When to call

    Problems that need a plumber

    Call a plumber if the shutoff valves will not close, if water is coming from the faucet body, or if the sink cabinet is damp and you cannot find the source. Call too if you strip a fitting or crack the faucet base while working.

    A plumber can replace the faucet, the supply lines and the valves in one visit. This is often the cheapest long-term fix when the faucet is old and the valves are stiff.

    Water pressure

    Low pressure that is not the faucet

    If every faucet in the house has weak flow, the problem is not the Grohe. Check the main valve, any pressure regulator and the meter valve. Partly closed valves are common after earlier work.

    If only the kitchen is weak, check the shutoff valves under the sink and the supply lines. A kinked line or a half-open valve can cut flow in a way that looks like a failed aerator.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Where do I find my Grohe model number?

    It may be on the underside of the faucet body, on the box or on the instruction sheet. If you cannot find it, a plumber can identify the cartridge.

    Can I use a generic cartridge?

    Use the part made for your model. A wrong cartridge may fit but leak or work poorly.

    Why does my pull-down hose not retract?

    The counterweight may have slipped or the hose may be kinked. Check under the sink.

    Should I worry about lead in an older faucet?

    The MWRA says lead can come from older brass faucets. Ask about testing, and consider replacing a faucet from before 1990.

    Does Revere water clog aerators with scale?

    Less often than hard water. The MWRA says its water is soft, so debris is the more likely cause.
