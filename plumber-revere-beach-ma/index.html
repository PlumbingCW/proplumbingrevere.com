The neighborhood

The first public beach and the buildings that grew up beside it

The National Park Service describes Revere Beach as the first ocean beach in the United States acquired for public recreation. The Metropolitan Park System took it over in 1895, and the Metropolitan Park Commission redesigned it under landscape architect Charles Eliot.

Wonderland amusement park opened in 1906, and the Massachusetts Historical Commission notes that urban renewal later removed nearly all of the historic fabric, leaving cast iron pavilions. Houses in Mission Revival and Moderne styles still stand along the Boulevard.

Because the Boulevard has been rebuilt more than once, buildings of very different ages stand side by side. Do not assume what a pipe is made of from the street. A plumber checks inside.

Local note

A beach that was rebuilt, and why it matters for buildings

Wikipedia reports that the Blizzard of 1978 destroyed many remaining businesses and that a long revitalization effort led to the beach officially reopening in May 1992. The city is also working on a new Revere High School on the former Wonderland Greyhound Park land, with ground broken in August 2025.

We include this as context only. It says the area has changed a great deal and that building ages vary. It does not tell us the condition of any pipe, so we make no claim about one.

Sources: National Park Service Revere Beach Reservation Historic District page. Massachusetts Historical Commission Revere community report (1981, so dated). Wikipedia article on Revere, Massachusetts.

Before the plumber arrives

Steps to take for any active leak

Shut the nearest valve, or the main if needed. Keep power off in wet areas and move belongings away from the water. Take photos for the landlord, association or insurer.

Tell the neighbors below. Water moves down quickly in buildings that share walls and floors.

FAQ

Plumbing questions in Revere Beach

Do you serve the Revere Beach Boulevard area?

Yes. We take calls near Revere Beach from our base on Revere Beach Parkway. Call (617) 752-0573 and the dispatcher will confirm timing for your address.

Is Revere Beach its own neighborhood?

It is a district named for the beach and the Boulevard, and it overlaps several neighborhoods. Tell us the cross street when you call so we place you correctly.

Who approves plumbing work in a condo?

The association rules vary, so ask management. Work that touches shared pipes, risers or shutoffs usually needs approval. An urgent leak should still be stopped at once.

Can you locate a leak without opening walls?

Often, yes. Leak detection tools can narrow down the source so that less of the wall or ceiling needs opening. See our leak detection page for the process.

Do I need a permit for plumbing work in Revere?

Many jobs do. According to the state plumbers and gas fitters board, permits go only to licensed plumbers. Ask the plumber whether your job needs one before work starts.
