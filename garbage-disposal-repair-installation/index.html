Emergency garbage disposal repair in Revere, MA covers a disposal that hums but will not spin, a leak under the sink and a unit that has stopped working. It also covers a clogged sink drain and the installation of a new disposal.

If a disposal is leaking, switch off its power at the wall switch and the breaker, then close the sink shutoff valves. Call the 24/7 line, and a licensed plumber can come the same day when available.

Symptoms

What each disposal problem usually means

Humming but not spinning

The blades are jammed. Turn the power off before anything else.

Silent, no hum

The unit has lost power. Often a tripped reset button or a breaker.

Leak under the sink

A loose drain connection, a cracked housing or a failed flange seal.

Bad smell and slow drain

Residue in the drain line, not just in the disposal.

Safety

Disposal jammed: safe steps before you call

A jammed disposal can be freed safely if the power is off, and it is dangerous if the power is on. Never put a hand in the unit. The 24/7 line is answered every hour if you want a plumber to handle it.

Power off first

Turn off the wall switch and the circuit breaker so the unit cannot start.

Use the hex key

Many units have a hex socket on the underside. Turn it back and forth to free the blades.

Press the reset

The red button on the bottom resets an overloaded motor once the jam is cleared.

Call if water leaks

A leak means close the shutoffs and call the line.

Plumbing side

What goes down a Revere kitchen drain matters

A disposal grinds food but does not make grease safe. Grease cools in the line and builds up, especially in older apartment buildings where the kitchen drain runs a long way to the stack. Many Revere kitchens connect to cast iron that is already rough inside.

Run cold water with the unit and clear the drain regularly. If the kitchen sink keeps slowing after the disposal is fixed, the cause is in the line, and drain cleaning or hydro jetting is the next step.

Electrical and rules

Electrical and permit points

A disposal needs a switched outlet or a hardwired connection. If the sink has no outlet, a licensed electrician may need to add one. Massachusetts electrical work follows 527 CMR 12.00, which is based on the National Electrical Code (NEC).

In Revere, the Building Division issues plumbing permits to licensed plumbers only, so ask the plumber whether a simple unit swap needs one. Rules vary by job, so confirm with the Building Division.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle garbage disposal emergencies at night or on weekends in Revere?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day. A plumber can be sent for a leaking or failed disposal, and the dispatcher tells you how to switch it off safely.

Can I install a garbage disposal myself?

It is possible, but the electrical and drain connections must be right. A plumber also checks the sink and trap. For a rental, ask your landlord first.

Why does my disposal hum and not spin?

Something is jamming the blades. Turn the power off and free it with the hex key, or call a plumber.

Why does my kitchen sink still drain slowly after the repair?

The clog may be in the pipe past the disposal. A drain cleaning visit can clear it.

What size disposal should I choose?

A half to three-quarter horsepower unit suits most homes. Heavier use suggests a larger motor.

Will the dishwasher still work?

Yes. The plumber reconnects the dishwasher drain and opens the knockout plug if the unit is new.
