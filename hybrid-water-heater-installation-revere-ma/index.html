Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Revere, MA

A hybrid water heater, also called a heat pump water heater, pulls heat from the air around it to warm the water, and it uses electric elements as a backup. It needs open space, a way to drain condensate and the right electrical supply. In return it uses much less electricity than a standard electric tank.

Many Revere basements can host one. A plumber checks the room before you buy, and for planned work this is a good project to book early.

    How it works

    What a hybrid water heater does differently

    A standard electric tank heats water with elements that turn electricity into heat directly. A hybrid unit has a small heat pump on top that moves heat from the room air into the tank, which takes less electricity for the same hot water. The elements switch on only when demand is high or the room is very cold.

    The heat pump also cools and dries the air a little, which can be a benefit in a damp basement. In a small closet it can make the space cold, so the room matters.

    Checklist

    Four things a basement must offer

    Open air

    The unit needs enough surrounding air. Check the maker's minimum, and avoid a tight closet unless it is vented.

    Condensate drain

    The heat pump makes water that must drain to a floor drain or a pump.

    Electrical supply

    Most models need a dedicated circuit. A plumber and an electrician confirm what you have.

    Height and clearance

    The unit is taller than a standard tank. Measure ceiling height and stairs.

    Local note

    Local note: the Mass Save rebate and Revere permit

    According to Mass Save, a qualifying ENERGY STAR heat pump water heater earns $750 per unit, and a split-system heat pump water heater earns $1,500. A 120V model on a 15-amp circuit, rated UEF 2.20, also earns $750. The equipment must be new and installed by a licensed plumber in a one to four unit home, replacing an electric, propane, gas or oil heater.

    The page lists installation between January 1 and December 31, 2026, with documents due by February 28, 2027. It gives an example average install cost of $3,200, or $2,450 after the rebate, and says the rebate cannot be combined with other utility offers for the same equipment. Terms can change, so read the current page before you buy.

    Revere requires a plumbing permit for a water heater replacement, and the Building Division issues plumbing permits only to licensed plumbers through its online portal. A homeowner cannot apply.

    Sources: Mass Save residential heat pump water heater page (utility program) and the City of Revere Building Division page (municipal source).

    Rental and condo notes

    If you rent or own a condo

    In a rental, the landlord usually owns the heater, so a tenant should ask rather than buy. In a condo, the association may control where the unit can sit and how it vents. Ask before you order.

    If your old tank has already failed, the choice shifts. A same-day replacement may use a standard tank, and a hybrid install may come later. See emergency water heater replacement for that path, and our Chelsea page if you live next door.

    Planning

    Questions to ask before you order

    Ask the installer how much clearance the unit needs on every side and how the condensate will drain. Ask whether your electrical panel has room for a new circuit. Ask what happens to your hot water while the work is done.

    Ask for the model number in writing so you can check it against the rebate list before you pay. Keep the invoice, the model and serial numbers and the permit record together, because the rebate form asks for them.

    Running costs

    What to expect after the install

    A hybrid unit usually offers a standard mode, an efficiency mode and a mode that uses only the elements. The efficiency mode saves the most but heats more slowly. Choose the mode that fits your household, and ask the installer to show you how to change it.

    Keep the air filter clean if the model has one, and check the condensate drain once a year for slime or blockage. A plumber can do both during a yearly visit.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    Is a hybrid water heater noisy?

    It makes a fan and compressor sound similar to a dehumidifier. In a basement it is usually fine. Avoid placing it next to a bedroom wall.

    Will it make my basement cold?

    It cools the room a little. In a large basement that is minor. In a small closet it can be noticeable.

    Can I use a hybrid unit with no floor drain?

    You can use a condensate pump to lift the water to a drain. Ask the plumber about it.

    Does Mass Save pay for installation?

    The rebate is a payment toward the equipment cost. It does not cover installation, and it cannot exceed the purchase price.

    Is there an Inflation Reduction Act tax credit?

    The Mass Save page says several federal credits expired at the end of 2025. Ask a tax professional about any other incentives.
