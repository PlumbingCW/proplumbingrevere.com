Service line leak: what to do first

A service line leak can wash out soil, flood a basement wall and waste water through the meter. It is urgent because the water keeps flowing until the line is closed.

Close the main inside

Turn the main valve clockwise. If it will not turn, call the city and the plumber.

Watch the meter

If usage still registers after the main is closed, the leak is upstream of the house valve.

Do not dig yourself

Buried gas, electric and water lines are near each other. Digging needs Dig Safe.

Call the line

The 24/7 dispatcher records the address and sends a plumber.

Lead

Lead service lines in Revere: the facts on record

According to the MWRA, its reservoir water is lead-free, and lead enters tap water from lead service lines, lead solder or older brass faucets. In its 2025 results, the MWRA reported that Revere lists 362 lead service lines, 254 of unknown material, 23 galvanized lines requiring replacement and 10,427 non-lead lines.

Revere removed 62 lead services in 2025 and plans to remove 80 more in 2026. The MWRA offers zero-interest loans and a 25 percent grant for lead service line replacement, with a goal of full replacement by 2032. Ask the Revere Water Department at 781-286-8145 what the city has recorded for your address.

Meters

Meters, bills and who handles what

Revere has been replacing meters citywide with electronic Sensus iPERL units. According to the city, the replacement is mandatory, residential customers pay nothing directly and the register connects to a radio mounted outside. A leak detection system on the street mains gives the city near-immediate notice of main leaks.

The Water Department handles the main and the meter. The city page does not say who maintains the line between the main and your property, so ask the department at 781-286-8145 where responsibility starts for your address before you commission work.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle water service line emergencies at night or on weekends in Revere?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day. A licensed plumber can be dispatched when a service line is leaking, and the dispatcher helps you close the main.

Is my water service line made of lead?

It may be if the house is old. Ask the Revere Water Department at 781-286-8145 what the inventory lists, and ask a plumber to check the pipe where it enters the basement.

Can I get help paying to replace a lead line?

The MWRA offers zero-interest loans and a 25 percent grant for lead service line replacement. Ask the MWRA at 617-242-5323 for current terms.

Who is responsible for the line from the street?

The city page does not state it. Ask the Revere Water and Sewer Department before you commission work.

Will the street be dug up?

Sometimes. Trenchless methods can reduce digging. The plumber explains the options.

How do I know the problem is the service line?

Sudden low pressure, a wet spot in the yard or a high bill can all point to it. A plumber locates the leak before digging.
