According to the 2020 Census, Revere had 62,186 residents and Chelsea had 40,787. Revere sits in Suffolk County on the north shore of Boston Harbor. Winthrop lies to the southeast, Chelsea to the southwest and East Boston to the south. Everett and Malden are to the west, and Saugus and Lynn to the north.

Inside Revere we cover Beachmont, Shirley Avenue, West Revere and Squire Road, Oak Island and Point of Pines, plus the streets around Wonderland station and Revere Beach Boulevard. Revere uses ZIP code 02151.

Chelsea has its own page because it is a separate city with its own water and sewer department, its own housing and its own flooding history. Read the Chelsea emergency plumber page for the local detail.

We also dispatch to Everett (ZIP 02149), Winthrop (02152), Malden (02148), Saugus (01906) and East Boston (02128). These are nearby ZIP codes we serve, and we call them nearby because they are outside Revere itself. Call the emergency line from any of them and a dispatcher will confirm the next available plumber.

Two natural features shape plumbing on both sides of the line. The Rumney Marsh Reservation is a state park of about 600 acres shared by Revere and Saugus. Belle Isle Marsh borders Beachmont, and Mill Creek runs along the Revere and Chelsea boundary. Low ground near water is where basements flood and sump pumps work hardest.
