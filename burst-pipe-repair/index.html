When a burst pipe is an emergency: the first five minutes

Any pipe that is spraying or running without stopping is an emergency. The goal is to stop the water at the source and then limit damage. Here is what to do, in this order.

Close the main valve

Turn the main shutoff clockwise. In most Revere houses it is in the basement near the meter. In a multi-unit building, find the building main.

Causes

Why pipes burst in Revere houses

Freezing is the best-known cause. Water expands as it freezes and splits the pipe wall, and the break often shows as the ice thaws. Exposed pipes in unheated basements, garages and exterior walls are the most at risk during New England cold snaps.

Age is the other cause. According to Revere's housing plan, most homes were built before 1960, and old galvanized pipes can rust through at a joint without freezing at all. A pinhole leak can become a burst under water pressure.

Drying and insurance

After the pipe is fixed: drying out and claims

A repaired pipe is only half the job. Wet drywall and insulation can grow mold if they stay damp. Water damage restoration is a separate trade, and the plumber can tell you which areas are wet.

Many homeowners policies cover sudden burst pipe damage and exclude gradual leaks. Report the loss quickly, photograph everything and keep the plumber's invoice. If you rent, tell your landlord and your renter's insurer.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Revere?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year. A licensed plumber can arrive the same day when available, and the dispatcher helps you find and close the shutoff.

Where is my main water shutoff?

In most Revere houses, near the water meter in the basement. In a multi-unit building, there may be a building main and separate unit valves. Find it before an emergency.

Should I turn off the power if water is leaking?

Only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground. If not, stay out and call.

How do I prevent frozen pipes?

Insulate pipes in unheated spaces, seal drafts and keep a slow drip on exposed lines during a hard freeze. Know where your main is.

Will insurance cover a burst pipe?

Many policies cover sudden burst damage. Wear and gradual leaks are usually excluded. Ask your insurer.

How long does a burst pipe repair take?

A simple repair may take a few hours. Multiple breaks or hidden pipes take longer.
