Types

Why faucets drip and what actually wears out

Most faucets drip because an internal part has worn. Compression faucets wear washers and seats. Cartridge and ball faucets wear seals and O-rings. Ceramic disc faucets wear their discs or the seals around them.

A plumber identifies the type, checks the valve seat and replaces the worn part or the whole fixture. For a faucet that is more than about fifteen years old or has pitted finishes, replacement often makes more sense than repeated repairs.

When it will not stop

When a faucet or valve will not shut off

A fixture that cannot be turned off is an emergency, because water keeps flowing until something is closed upstream. Here is what to do while you wait. The 24/7 line is answered every hour.

Try the fixture shutoff

Turn the small valve on the supply under the sink or behind the fixture clockwise.

Use the main if needed

If the valve breaks or will not move, close the main water valve, usually near the meter.

Do not force a seized valve

Old valves can snap. Let a plumber replace them.

Open a low faucet

After closing the main, open a faucet on the lowest floor to drain the pipes and relieve pressure.

Lead and old brass

Older faucets and what the MWRA says about lead

According to the MWRA, its reservoir water is lead-free, and lead can enter tap water from lead service lines, lead solder or older brass faucets. If your Revere house or apartment still has a fixture from before 1990, that is a good reason to replace it rather than patch it.

The MWRA also adjusts the pH of its water to about 9.0 to 9.5 to reduce lead and copper leaching. Ask the city or the MWRA about testing your tap if you have an older home or a young child. A plumber can replace the fixture and the supply lines in one visit.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle faucet emergencies at night or on weekends in Revere?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day. A licensed plumber can come for a faucet or valve that will not shut off or a supply line that is spraying water.

Can you repair a Grohe or other branded kitchen faucet?

Yes. Many Grohe, Delta, Moen and Kohler parts are available. If a part is no longer made, the plumber can recommend a replacement.

Why does my faucet have low water pressure?

A clogged aerator, a partly closed stop valve or a failing cartridge is the usual cause. Low pressure in the whole house is a different problem.

How long does a faucet repair take?

Most take under an hour. New valves or corroded fittings add time.

Is it worth replacing the shutoff valves?

Often yes. Old valves can seize or leak, and a new one costs less than an emergency.

Should I replace an old brass faucet?

Consider it. The MWRA notes lead can come from older brass faucets, so replacing one from before 1990 is reasonable.
