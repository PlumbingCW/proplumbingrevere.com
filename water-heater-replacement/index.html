Emergency Water Heater Replacement in Revere, MA

Emergency water heater replacement in Revere, MA means removing a failed tank and installing a new one, usually the same day. A heater that leaks from the shell, rusts through or has stopped heating for good cannot be repaired.

Call the 24/7 line and a licensed plumber can plan the swap. Until then, close the cold water valve above the heater and shut off its power or gas. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 or National Grid at 1-800-233-5325.

Failure

When a water heater failure is an emergency

A tank that is leaking onto a finished floor, a heater in a rental unit with no hot water in winter or a gas heater that smells of gas all justify a same-day call. Water damage grows by the hour, and a flooded basement in Revere can reach a neighbor's unit.

If the tank is rusted through or the leak comes from the shell, replacement is the only fix. If the leak is from a valve or fitting, a plumber may still be able to repair it, so call before you decide.

Process

How a same-day replacement runs

The plumber works from the existing connections where code allows.

1

Choose the unit

The plumber confirms fuel type, tank size and venting, and agrees the model with you before ordering or pulling it from stock.

2

Drain and remove

The old tank is drained, disconnected and carried out. Gas lines are capped safely and tested.

3

Set and connect

The new tank is set on a pan or stand, connected to water, vent and fuel, and fitted with a relief valve and discharge pipe.

4

Fire up and test

The plumber checks combustion or elements, temperature, leaks and venting, then explains the controls.

Rebates

Mass Save rebates for heat pump water heaters

Mass Save offers a rebate of $750 per unit on qualifying ENERGY STAR heat pump water heaters, and $1,500 for a split-system heat pump water heater. The equipment must be installed by a licensed plumber in a home of one to four units and replace an existing electric, propane, gas or oil heater.

According to Mass Save, equipment must be installed between January 1 and December 31, 2026, and documents must be received by February 28, 2027. Its example uses a $3,200 average installation cost, or $2,450 after the rebate. Terms change, so check Mass Save before you buy and keep your invoice.

Permit

Permits and who may do the work

According to the state plumbers and gas fitters board, the local plumbing inspector must issue a permit before a water heater is replaced, and permits go only to licensed plumbers. The work follows 248 CMR 10.00, the Uniform State Plumbing Code. Revere's Building Division, in the American Legion Building basement at 249R Broadway, handles plumbing and gas permits through its online portal.

A licensed plumber signs the permit application. That protects you, because inspected work is easier to defend to an insurer or a buyer. You can check any plumber's license with the state board at 617-727-9952.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle water heater replacement emergencies at night or on weekends in Revere?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day. A dispatcher can arrange a plumber for a failed tank outside business hours, and same-day replacement is offered when stock and schedule allow.

How long does a replacement take?

A straightforward tank swap often takes a few hours. Venting changes, gas line work or relocating the unit add time.

How do I know the old heater is beyond repair?

A shell leak or a rusted-out tank bottom ends a tank. A tank near the end of its usual 8 to 12 year life with a large repair quote also points to replacement.

Will the old heater be taken away?

Yes. Removal and disposal of the old tank are part of the job.

Can I get the Mass Save heat pump rebate?

If the heater qualifies, is installed by a licensed plumber in a one to four unit home and meets the program dates, yes. Check the current terms at Mass Save.

Do I have to be home?

An adult should be present to give access and approve the price. Tell the dispatcher if the heater is in a shared basement.
