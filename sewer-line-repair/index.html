Responsibility

Who owns the sewer line in Revere

According to the Revere Water and Sewer Department, each property needs its own sewer service, running from the street sewer main to the house plumbing. As the property owner, you are responsible for keeping that service in good working order at your own expense.

That means the broken section between your foundation and the street is yours to fix. If the plumber's camera shows the break is in the public main, call the city at 311 (781-286-8311) so the Water and Sewer Department can handle it.

Backup response

Sewage backup in a basement: first steps

Raw sewage carries bacteria. A backup is both a health problem and an emergency repair. The 24/7 line is answered every hour, and a plumber can be sent the same day when available.

Stop water use

No flushing, showers or laundry until the line is open.

Protect yourself

Wear rubber boots and gloves. Keep children and pets away from the affected area.

Move belongings

Lift valuables and boxes off the floor if it is safe.

Call and document

Photograph the backup for insurance, then call the line.

Signs

What a failing sewer lateral looks like

Repeated backups, slow drains in several fixtures and gurgling when other drains run are the usual signs. A sewer smell in the yard or a sunken wet patch over the line can mean the pipe has broken underground.

In older Revere houses the original lateral may be clay or cast iron. Both can crack, shift or fill with roots over decades, especially where the soil is wet. On low ground near the marsh at Oak Island and Point of Pines, wet soil adds to the strain on old joints.

Rules

Permits, digging safely and what the city prohibits

Sewer work needs a permit from the Revere Building Division, which issues plumbing permits only to licensed plumbers. Before any digging, a 811 Dig Safe request marks buried utilities. Your plumber should arrange it.

The city also prohibits connecting downspouts, foundation drains or other surface or groundwater sources to the sewer service. Revere's Inflow Removal Project 10A, which the city lists as complete in July 2025, redirected private sources such as roof leaders and sump pumps. If your sump pump discharges into the sewer, fix that when you repair the lateral.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Revere?

Yes. The line is answered around the clock. A licensed plumber can be dispatched for sewage backups, and the dispatcher tells you what to do while you wait.

Do I need to repair the sewer line or can I clean it?

If the camera shows buildup only, cleaning may be enough. If it shows cracks, a break or sagging, the pipe needs repair.

How long does a sewer repair take?

Lining may take a day. Dig and replace may take several days with restoration.

Will the city pay for my sewer lateral?

No. The property owner is responsible for the service from the main to the house. The city is responsible for the public main.

Do you need a permit to repair a sewer line in Revere?

Yes. Plumbing permits are issued to licensed plumbers by the Building Division. The plumber applies for it.

How do I know the problem is not the city main?

If a camera shows your lateral is intact and neighbors also back up, call Revere 311 at 781-286-8311.
