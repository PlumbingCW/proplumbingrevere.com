Sizing

Choosing the right water heater for your household

The right tank depends on how many people use hot water at once. A single person in a Beachmont condo needs less capacity than a family sharing a bathroom and a laundry on the same morning. The plumber asks about showers, dishwashers and washing machines before recommending a size.

Fuel is the next decision. Gas heaters recover quickly but need a good vent and a correctly sized gas line. Electric tanks suit units without gas. Heat pump models use less electricity but need floor space, open air and a drain for condensate.

Basement reality

Installing in older Revere basements

Many Revere buildings predate modern code. A heater may sit in a basement that floods, beside a chimney flue that no longer matches the appliance or on a floor with no drain. The plumber checks these points before choosing a replacement.

In Beachmont and near Point of Pines, the city flood-readiness guidance points to low basements and storm surge. Raising the heater on a stand, adding a pan or choosing a spot above the likely water line protects the new unit. A sump pump helps keep the floor dry around it.

Rebates and permit

Rebates and permit rules that apply in 2026

According to Mass Save, a qualifying ENERGY STAR heat pump water heater earns a $750 rebate when a licensed plumber installs it in a one to four unit home between January 1 and December 31, 2026. A split-system heat pump water heater earns $1,500. Rebates cannot exceed the purchase price.

Revere requires a permit for plumbing and gas work and issues it only to licensed plumbers. Homeowners cannot apply. The permit gives you an inspection record you can show an insurer or a future buyer.

FAQ

Common questions

How long does a water heater installation take?

A like-for-like tank install often takes a few hours. Moving the unit, changing fuel or adding venting takes longer.

Can you install a hybrid heat pump water heater in a Revere basement?

Often yes, if the space has enough open air, clearance and a way to drain condensate. The plumber checks before recommending one.

Do I need a permit to install a water heater in Revere?

Yes, plumbing and gas work needs a permit from the Revere Building Division, and only a licensed plumber can apply. The plumber handles it.

Is a water softener needed with a new heater in Revere?

Usually not. The MWRA describes its water as soft, at about 16 mg/L of hardness, so scale builds slowly.

Can I upgrade from gas to electric or the other way?

Yes, but it can need new venting, a gas line or a new circuit. The plumber explains the extra work before you decide.

Do you offer same-day installation?

Same-day appointments are offered when available. If your heater has already failed, call the emergency line instead.
