Is It Easy to Install a Toilet? What Revere, MA Homes Need

Installing a toilet is simple when the floor is solid and the floor flange is in good shape. It is heavy work, and a bad wax ring or a cracked flange causes leaks that rot floors. In many older Revere homes, those hidden parts are where the trouble is.

If you can lift 60 to 100 pounds with a helper and the old toilet came off cleanly, a like-for-like swap is a reasonable project. If anything looks soft, cracked or corroded, call a plumber.

    Overview

    The main steps of a toilet install

    1

    Shut off and drain

    Close the supply valve, flush and sponge the remaining water from the tank and bowl.

    2

    Remove the old toilet

    Disconnect the supply, remove the bolt caps and nuts and lift the toilet straight up.

    3

    Inspect the flange

    Check the flange for cracks, rot and the right height. Replace damaged parts.

    4

    Set and tighten

    Place a new wax ring, lower the toilet over the bolts and tighten the nuts evenly.

    Pitfalls

    Where do-it-yourself installs go wrong

    The flange is the most common problem. If it sits below the floor, a wax ring may not seal. If it is cracked, the toilet rocks. A rocking toilet breaks the seal and leaks onto the floor, sometimes for weeks before a stain appears.

    Tightening bolts unevenly can crack the porcelain. The supply line and shutoff valve are also weak points. A seized valve on an old supply can snap while you turn it.

    Local note

    Local note: permits and who may do plumbing work in Revere

    The Revere Building Division states that plumbing and gas permits can only be issued to Massachusetts licensed plumbers, and that homeowners cannot apply for them. The division issues them through its online e-permitting portal.

    Whether a simple like-for-like toilet swap needs a permit is a question for the division, because the rule can depend on the work. Call the Building Division at 781-286-8196 before you start, or hire a licensed plumber who handles the permit.

    The state plumbers and gas fitters board says only a licensed master or journeyman plumber may do plumbing work at a home or business, and the board confirms licenses at 617-727-9952. Ask about both rules before you commit.

    Sources: City of Revere Building Division page (municipal source) and the Massachusetts plumbers and gas fitters consumer fact sheet (state source).

    Old houses

    What to expect in an older Revere bathroom

    According to Revere's housing plan, most homes were built before 1960 and the largest number before 1940. In many such houses, the bathroom has been remodeled in stages, so the toilet may sit on a patched floor with a flange that does not match.

    Measure the rough-in, the distance from the finished wall to the center of the drain, before you buy. Common sizes fit most toilets, but an odd one makes a new toilet impossible to set without moving the flange. If you rent, which about 51 percent of Revere households do, ask the landlord before you buy or replace anything.

    Tools and parts

    What to have on hand before you lift the old toilet

    You need a new wax ring or a wax-free seal, closet bolts, a new supply line, a putty knife, an adjustable wrench and a level. A few old towels and a bucket help with the water left in the trap.

    Plan where the old toilet will go. A full toilet can weigh as much as a small appliance, so clear the path and protect the floor. Cover the open drain with a rag while you work so sewer gas stays out of the room.

    After the install

    Test it before you walk away

    Turn the water on slowly and let the tank fill. Check the supply line connection for drips by running dry tissue around it. Flush several times and look at the base for any water.

    Tighten the tank bolts only a little, then check again after a day. Caulk the base only if you want to, and leave a small gap at the back so a future leak can show itself.

    Keep the old wax ring out of the trash if it is not needed for a test fit. Replace it once you are sure the toilet sits right.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    How long does it take to install a toilet?

    A plumber often finishes in an hour or two. A first-time DIY install can take half a day with the cleanup.

    Do I need to replace the wax ring?

    Yes, every time you lift the toilet. A used ring will not seal reliably.

    Can I install a toilet in a Revere rental?

    Ask the landlord first. Many leases require the landlord to arrange plumbing work.

    Can a homeowner pull a plumbing permit in Revere?

    No. The Revere Building Division says plumbing permits go only to licensed plumbers. Ask the division whether your job needs one.

    What if the toilet rocks after I set it?

    Do not use shims alone. Check the flange and the floor. A rocking toilet needs a plumber or a repair, and our Chelsea page covers the next city over.
