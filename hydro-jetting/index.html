Emergency Hydro Jetting in Revere, MA

Emergency hydro jetting in Revere, MA uses a high-pressure water jet to scour grease, scale and roots from the inside of a drain or sewer line. It is the next step when a cable only opens a hole through the clog and the backup returns.

Technique

What hydro jetting does that a cable cannot

A cable drills a hole through a clog. A jetter sends water at high pressure through a hose with a nozzle that scours the whole inside wall of the pipe. Grease layers, scale and soft roots are stripped and flushed downstream.

The result is a pipe close to its original diameter. That matters in older Revere buildings, where decades of buildup narrow cast iron drains and make every future clog likelier.

Backup response

Sewer backup: what to do first

When sewage comes up in a basement drain or a tub, the line is blocked past the point of the fixture. Hydro jetting may be the right fix, but only a plumber can tell after looking inside.

Stop adding water

Do not flush, shower or run machines. Anything you add must go somewhere.

Keep out of the water

Backup water carries bacteria. Wear boots and gloves, and keep children and pets out.

Turn off basement electric

Only if you can reach the panel while standing on dry ground. Otherwise stay out and call.

Limits

When jetting is not the right choice

Jetting is a cleaning method, not a repair. A pipe that is cracked, separated or collapsed needs sewer line repair, not more water. High pressure can also push water into a damaged joint and erode the soil around it.

That is why the plumber runs a camera first on repeat backups. If the camera shows a sound pipe with buildup, jetting is a good fit. If it shows damage, you get an honest explanation and repair options before any more work.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Revere?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day. A plumber can be dispatched when a line is backing up, and will usually inspect before jetting.

Is hydro jetting safe for old cast iron?

On sound pipe, yes. A camera first shows whether the pipe is intact. If it is thin or cracked, the plumber will recommend other options.

How long does jetting last?

It depends on what caused the buildup. Grease and scale stripped from the wall take time to return. Roots may return if the pipe is open.

Will jetting fix a broken sewer line?

No. It cleans pipe. A broken line needs repair or lining.

Can jetting be done in an apartment building?

Yes, with access to a cleanout. The plumber coordinates with the building and warns neighbors of possible noise.

Does the city sewer need jetting?

According to the Revere Water and Sewer Department, the property owner maintains the sewer service from the main to the house. The city handles the public main. If you suspect the main, call Revere 311 at 781-286-8311.
