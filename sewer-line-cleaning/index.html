Main line

Main sewer line vs branch drain: why it matters

A branch drain serves one fixture. The main line serves the whole building, so when it blocks, the lowest fixtures fail first. A basement floor drain that bubbles when you flush upstairs is a textbook sign.

Main line cleaning uses a heavier machine and longer cable than branch work. The plumber often needs the cleanout, a capped fitting usually in the basement or at the foundation. If none exists, the plumber can pull a toilet or install a cleanout so the line can be serviced again.

Main line blocked: what to do while you wait

A blocked main can overflow without warning, and it usually does so at the lowest drain. The line is answered 24 hours a day, so there is no need to wait until morning.

Locate the cleanout

Find the capped pipe in the basement or outside. Do not open it. Pressure and wastewater may be behind it.

Hold the laundry

Washing machines discharge large volumes. Wait.

Warn other units

In a two-family or triple-decker, tell neighbors so they stop using water too.

Call the line

Mention if the basement drain is bubbling or neighbors are affected.

Causes

What blocks a Revere sewer lateral

Tree roots

Small roots enter at joints and grow into a mat that catches paper.

Grease and wipes

Grease coats the pipe and wipes snag on rough spots.

Scale and corrosion

Old cast iron develops a rough interior that traps debris.

Roof and sump water

Illegal connections add flow that overloads the lateral. Revere's inflow project targets these.

City rules

What the city says about sewer services and storm water

According to Revere's Water and Sewer Department, connecting downspouts, foundation drains or other surface or groundwater sources to the sewer service is prohibited. The city's Inflow Removal Project 10A redirected private sources such as roof leaders and sump pumps, and lists 28 private inflow sources in that area.

A lateral that runs clean after cleaning but backs up during heavy rain may be taking in storm water. Tell the plumber, who can check where your roof drains and sump pump discharge. For clogged street catch basins, the city's 311 line at 781-286-8311 sends a crew.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle sewer line emergencies at night or on weekends in Revere?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day. A plumber can come when a main line is blocked, and the dispatcher tells you how to limit the damage.

How do I know it is the main line and not one drain?

If several fixtures back up at once or a floor drain bubbles when you run water above, the main line is blocked.

Can roots be removed without digging?

Often yes, with a cutting head. Roots may return if the pipe stays open at the joint, so a camera and a repair may follow.

What if my house has no cleanout?

A plumber can pull a toilet to access the line and can install a cleanout so future cleaning is easier.

Does the city clean my sewer lateral?

No. The property owner is responsible for the lateral. The city maintains the public main and catch basins.

How long does a main line cleaning take?

Many take one to two hours. Heavy roots or a collapsed pipe take longer.
