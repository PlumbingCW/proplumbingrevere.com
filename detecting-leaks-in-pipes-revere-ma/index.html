Detecting Leaks in Pipes: A Revere, MA Homeowner Guide

The simplest way to find a hidden pipe leak is the meter test. Turn off every tap and appliance, read the water meter, wait 30 minutes without using water and read it again. If the numbers moved, water is going somewhere you cannot see.

A moving meter does not tell you where the leak is. It tells you to stop guessing and to check fixtures, then call a plumber who can trace the pipe without tearing open walls.

    Meter test

    How to read your meter for a leak

    You need about 30 minutes and a flashlight. Use the same method each time so the readings compare.

    1

    Stop all use

    Close every tap, and pause the dishwasher, washer and ice maker.

    2

    Find the leak indicator

    Many meters have a small triangle or dial that spins when any water moves.

    3

    Record the reading

    Write down the full number, or note where the dial sits.

    4

    Wait and compare

    After 30 minutes with no use, read again. Any change means a leak.

    Clues

    Signs of a leak you cannot see

    A higher bill

    A jump with no change in how you live is a strong hint.

    Musty smell

    Damp air in a closet, cabinet or basement corner can mean a slow leak.

    Stains and bubbling paint

    Brown rings or soft paint on a wall or ceiling often sit below the source.

    Sound of running water

    Hissing or trickling when everything is off points to a pressurized line.

    Local note

    Local note: your meter and your bill in Revere

    The City of Revere Water and Sewer Department reads and bills for water, and the city maintains a meter program for residents. If you suspect a leak, call the department at 781-286-8145 and ask how to read your meter and how billing for a leak is handled.

    The department also states that the owner is responsible for the service pipe on their property. A leak on your side of the meter is yours to fix, so a quick call before you dig can save time.

    The MWRA supplies Revere with soft water at about 16 mg/L of hardness, so scale clogging pipes is less of a concern than in hard-water areas. A leak here is more often a joint, a fitting or an old fixture.

    Sources: City of Revere Water and Sewer Department pages and the MWRA hardness page (municipal and utility sources).

    Next steps

    What to do once you confirm a leak

    Shut off the main valve if water is spreading. If the meter stops moving when the main is closed, the leak is inside your plumbing. If the meter still moves with the main closed, the leak may be on the street side, and you should call the Water Department.

    A plumber uses acoustic listening, pressure tests and thermal imaging to narrow the spot. Compare it with burst pipe repair if a pipe has actually failed, and see our Chelsea emergency page if the leak is in the next city.

    Standards

    What a plumber follows when finding and fixing a leak

    According to the Massachusetts plumbing code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code, repairs to pressurized lines need approved materials and proper joints, and some work needs a permit and inspection. A plumber who finds a leak in a wall will open the smallest area needed and restore it so the pipe can be inspected.

    Ask for a clear price before any wall is opened. A plumber should tell you what was found, where it was and what will be done about it.

    Cost control

    Small habits that catch leaks early

    Read your meter once a month and write the number down. Look under sinks when you take out the trash. Check the area around the water heater each time you are in the basement.

    A stain that grows, a smell that returns or a bill that rises for no reason is worth a call. Early repairs usually cost far less than repairing a ceiling or a floor after a slow leak.

    Dye test

    Quick checks for the usual suspects

    Check the toilet flapper with dye first, because it is the most common hidden leak. Then look at the washing machine hoses, the ice maker line and the dishwasher supply. Rubber hoses split and bulge with age, and braided hoses should be replaced on a schedule.

    Run your hand under each sink and along the shutoff valves. A cold, damp feel is a sign even when the paper towel stays dry.

    FAQ

    Common questions

    How much water can a small leak waste?

    A steady drip adds up over weeks. The meter test shows whether it is a drip or something larger.

    Can I find a slab leak myself?

    You can notice warm spots or a moving meter. Pinpointing the pipe needs tools, and a plumber should do it.

    Does the meter test work with a toilet leak?

    Yes. A running toilet moves the meter. Check it first with a few drops of dye in the tank.

    Who do I call in Revere if the leak is before my meter?

    Call the Revere Water and Sewer Department at 781-286-8145. The owner is responsible for the pipe on their own property.

    Will a plumber have to cut my walls?

    Often not at first. Listening and thermal tools narrow the spot so any opening is small.
