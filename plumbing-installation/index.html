Scope

What plumbing installation includes

An installation job starts with a plan for where water comes in and where waste goes out. That means rough-in pipes behind walls, then connecting fixtures such as toilets, sinks, tubs, showers, washing machines and dishwashers.

The plumber also sets shutoff valves, vents, traps and drains. Vent pipes are easy to forget and cause gurgling and slow drains if they are missing or wrong.

Basement work

Adding plumbing below grade in a flood-prone city

A basement bathroom sits below the sewer, so waste often has to be pumped up. That makes a sewage ejector pump and a backflow protection plan part of the design. In flood-prone parts of Revere, a basement fixture also needs a way to keep street water from entering the house.

The city flood-readiness page says that basement fixtures such as sinks, floor drains and washing machines can let sewage back up during street flooding, and it suggests hiring a professional plumber to install a backflow preventer. Ask about one before you finish the walls. See sump pump service for related protection.

Rules

Permits, licensing and inspections in Revere

According to the state plumbers and gas fitters board, a local plumbing inspector issues a permit before plumbing is installed, altered, removed, replaced or repaired, and permits go only to licensed plumbers. The Revere Building Division handles plumbing and gas permits online.

Revere sets permitted work hours from Monday to Saturday, 8 a.m. to 6 p.m. Work outside those hours needs a special permit from the Building Commissioner. In a condo or rental, the association or landlord may also need to approve the work.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a permit to add a bathroom in Revere?

Yes. Plumbing work needs a permit, and only a licensed plumber can apply through the Revere Building Division. The plumber files it.

Can you install a basement bathroom?

Yes. It usually needs an ejector pump and backflow protection, and the design should consider flood risk.

How long does a new bathroom plumbing install take?

Rough-in often takes a few days. Final fixture setting comes after the walls and floors are finished.

Can I buy the fixtures myself?

Yes. Share the model numbers early so the plumber can check rough-in dimensions and valve types.

Do you work with other trades?

Yes. We coordinate with your contractor and electrician. Plumbing work is ours, and we need access at the right stage.

Do you do emergency installs?

Same-day appointments are offered when available for urgent fixture installs. For a failed pipe, call the emergency line.
