The neighborhood

Bungalows, two-families and a commercial corridor

Much of Revere stayed undeveloped until after 1940. The Massachusetts Historical Commission describes modest suburban housing along Malden Street and Park Avenue, including concrete bungalows, and Craftsman-derived two-family houses from the 1920s. Park Avenue was subdivided along trolley routes in the early twentieth century.

Route 1 runs through the area as American Legion Highway and Squire Road. An early airport once stood on Squire Road at Rumney Marsh, on the site that later became the Northgate Shopping Center.

Houses of this era may have had work done in later decades. A plumber checks what is actually behind the wall, not what the age suggests.

Local note

Rumney Marsh, Route 1 and the city flood sensors

Rumney Marsh Reservation is a state park of about 600 acres shared by Revere and Saugus. The City of Revere flood-readiness page lists a Hohonu flood sensor at Rumney Marsh and offers High Water and King Tide text alerts.

Inland streets are not the same as the shoreline blocks, but low spots exist, and a storm can still overload a street drain. Report drainage problems outside your property to Revere 311 at 781-286-8311.

Sources: Massachusetts Historical Commission Revere community report (1981, so dated). Wikipedia neighborhood summary for Revere. City of Revere flood-readiness page (municipal source).

Homes and businesses

What West Revere callers tend to need checked

General guidance, not a finding about any one building.

Water heater age

A tank that is old, rusting or leaking at the base should be inspected before it fails. Note the age from the label.

Main line drains

Slow drains in several fixtures at once point to the main line rather than one trap.

Hidden leaks

An unexplained jump in the water bill may be a leak. Check the meter with every fixture off.

Commercial fixtures

Hotels, restaurants and plazas have different loads and schedules. Ask about work outside business hours.

Planning

Planned work or urgent work: how to decide

If water is spreading, shut the main valve and call. If a heater is old but still working, planned replacement is usually calmer and easier to schedule than a failure at night.

For a business, ask whether work can be staged around opening hours, and whether a permit or inspection is likely. Ask before the job starts so the schedule is clear.

FAQ

Plumbing questions in West Revere

Is West Revere an official neighborhood?

It is a name in common use for the western part of the city, and Wikipedia lists it as one of Revere's neighborhoods. Boundaries are informal, so tell us your street when you call.

Do you work on businesses along Squire Road?

Yes. Our commercial plumbing page describes the kinds of jobs we take. Tell the dispatcher what the business is and what has failed.

How do I check for a hidden leak?

Turn off every fixture, then read the water meter and watch for movement. If it moves, water is going somewhere. Call a plumber to locate it before opening walls.

Should I replace an old water heater before it fails?

Often yes. A planned replacement can be scheduled at a convenient time, while a failed tank can flood a floor. A plumber can inspect it and advise.

Do I need a permit for plumbing work in Revere?

Many jobs do. According to the state plumbers and gas fitters board, permits go only to licensed plumbers. Ask the plumber whether your job needs one before work starts.
