Emergency Sump Pump Repair in Revere, MA

Emergency sump pump repair in Revere, MA gets a failed pump working again or replaces it before a storm or a high tide fills the basement. A sump pump collects groundwater in a pit and pushes it away from the house, so a dead pump can mean water on the floor within hours.

Call the 24/7 line if the pit is full or the pump will not run. Stay out of a flooded basement with live electricity, and move valuables upstairs only if the floor is dry and safe.

Local risk

Why sump pumps matter more in Revere than in most towns

Revere sits between Revere Beach on the east and the Rumney Marsh Reservation on the north, with Belle Isle Marsh near Beachmont. According to the city flood-readiness page, Beachmont and the Point of Pines and Riverside area are the parts most exposed to flooding. The page recommends sump pumps and backflow preventers for basements.

The city also reports sea level rise projections for Boston Harbor of 4 to 8 inches by 2030 and 15 to 36 inches by 2070. Higher water tables press on basement walls and floors. A pump that struggled last year may not keep up this year.

Pump failure

Basement flooding: what to do first

If the pump has failed and water is rising, call the line first. A licensed plumber can arrive the same day when available. Until then, these steps keep you safe.

Stay out of deep water

Water near outlets, a furnace or a panel can electrify the room. Do not wade in.

Check the pump power

If the floor is dry, check the plug, the breaker and the float switch.

Bail or use a spare pump

A bucket or a utility pump can buy time if you can reach the pit safely.

Photograph everything

Take photos of the water line and damaged items for insurance.

3

4

Test and add backup

Water is poured in to test the float, and a battery backup can be added.

Discharge rules

Where the pump may and may not discharge

According to the Revere Water and Sewer Department, connecting downspouts, foundation drains or other surface or groundwater sources to the sanitary sewer is prohibited. The city's Inflow Removal Project 10A redirected private sources such as roof leaders and sump pumps away from the sewer.

That means your sump discharge should go outside, onto a part of the yard that drains away, not into a floor drain or sewer cleanout. If your old pump drains into the sewer, fix it when you replace the pump. A plumber can reroute the line.

Alerts

Using city alerts and moisture sensors

The city has installed Hohonu flood sensors at Mills Avenue, Rumney Marsh, Belle Isle Marsh and Pearl Avenue, and offers High Water and King Tide text alerts. The flood-readiness page also notes that some home security companies and sump pump makers offer moisture alerts for basements.

A water sensor near the pit costs little and can warn you before the floor is wet. Pair it with a backup pump if you live near the marsh or in a low block.

Callers in Beachmont and Point of Pines often ask about pumps, so each of those neighborhood pages lists local flood notes.

FAQ

Common questions

Do you handle sump pump emergencies at night or on weekends in Revere?

Yes. The line is answered 24 hours a day. A licensed plumber can come when a pump has failed and water is rising, and the dispatcher tells you what to do while you wait.

How often should a sump pump be replaced?

Many pumps last several years, though use and water quality matter. Test yours before wet seasons and replace it if it is noisy, slow or tripping.

Can my sump pump discharge into the sewer?

No. According to the Revere Water and Sewer Department, connecting sump pumps to the sanitary sewer is prohibited. Discharge outside, away from the foundation.

What is a battery backup for?

It keeps the pump running during a power outage, which often happens in storms and high tides.

Does Revere offer flood alerts?

Yes. The city has Hohonu sensors and offers High Water and King Tide text alerts. Check the city flood-readiness page to sign up.

Will a sump pump stop sewage backup?

No. A sump pump handles groundwater. Sewage backup needs a backflow preventer on the sewer line.
